# Arshad Electronics · demo 1

A static, self-contained demo of the new Arshad Electronics website: the Site Lab 3 redesign, opening on the picks of 16 October 2026. It is for client review and is not the live site.

## What's in this folder

- 13 pages, each in a folder at the same address as on arshadelectronics.com:
  - `index.html`: homepage
  - `corona-discharge-treaters/`: Fluxomatic, with `blown-cast-films/`, `woven-fabric/` and `3d-objects/`
  - `induction-cap-sealers/`: Fluxosealer, with `fluxosealer-brezo-series/` and `fluxosealer-aurae-series/`
  - `simco-ion-static-eliminators/`, `about-us/`, `clients/` (new), `faq/` and `contact-us/`
- `assets/`: the site's scripts and styles
- `img/` and `models/`: photos, logos and the 3D models
- `404.html` and `robots.txt`

## The look

- Warm paper colours with the logo olive, on a linen texture.
- Section sheets that layer over each other as you scroll, and a layered wipe between pages.
- Every product page opens the same way: the product picture, its description and buttons for each series.
- Product pictures are the photos from arshadelectronics.com, with series thumbnails on Blown / cast film and Brezo.

The **Options** tab on the right edge opens a small drawer with three tabs:
- **Colour:** palettes and accents.
- **Surface:** solid colour or one of 11 textures.
- **Product images:** the site photos, or the 3D models and drawn images.

Choices are remembered on that device only.

## Put it on GitHub Pages

1. Upload the **contents** of this folder (not the folder itself) to the repository root, keeping the subfolders.
2. In the repository, go to **Settings › Pages**. Under *Build and deployment*, choose **Deploy from a branch**, then branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`. Save.
3. After a minute the site is at `https://<owner>.github.io/<repository>/`.

Every link is relative, so the site works at that sub-address, on a custom domain, or from any web server. It needs a web server: opening `index.html` straight from disk will not load the 3D models.

## Things to know before sharing

- **Forms are not connected.** The contact and enquiry forms thank the visitor and say the forms are not connected yet. Nothing is sent. Call, email and WhatsApp links work.
- **Hidden from search engines.** Every page carries `noindex, nofollow`, so this copy does not compete with arshadelectronics.com.
- **Content.** Only figures published on arshadelectronics.com are shown.
- **Languages.** English, Spanish and Russian, switched with the flags at the top right. A link can open a language directly with `?lang=es` or `?lang=ru`. The translations have not yet been checked by a native speaker.
- **Internet needed.** The 3D engine (three.js) loads from cdnjs and the fonts from Google Fonts.

## Rebuilding

This folder is generated by `python 3d/demo1/build_demo1.py`, which builds Site Lab 3 (`3d/lab3`) on the draft 1 build (`3d/draft-build`, sources in `3d/web`) and applies the demo 1 picks.
